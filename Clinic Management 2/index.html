Terms, Privacy & Patient Rights

1. Clinical Appointment Terms

Appointments scheduled through MedPulse are subject to doctor emergency triage priority. In case of emergency surgeries, the clinic will notify the patient promptly and offer alternative priority slots.

2. Payment and Cancellation Policy

Online UPI fees verified against screenshot uploads are non-refundable if cancelled less than 2 hours before the scheduled slot. Offline payments are accepted in cash or card at the clinic front desk.

3. Medical Records & E-Prescription Confidentiality

All prescriptions uploaded and generated on MedPulse are protected under strict medical confidentiality protocols. QR verification is provided for certified dispensing pharmacies.
